4 · Ranked upgrades

The first three are the do-these-today list. Effort is rough: S = under an hour, M = half a day.

Do these first — biggest impact, smallest diff

1 · Fix the sizes attribute. 100vw → the real cell width. One line. Takes the portfolio from ~1.3 MB to ~600 KB with no new assets — the 400w files already exist (18-img-0043-400w.webp is 27 KB vs 323 KB at 1200w). Downscaling the current pick to the true render box scores PSNR 40–47 dB, which is visually lossless. S Fix: src/rows.ts sizes attribute.

2 · Move grayscale out of @media (hover:hover). Your brand is monochrome on desktop and colour on phones — 25 of 50 frames are genuinely colour. Keep the hover reveal behind the media query; move the base treatment out of it. S Fix: src/styles.css:237-241.

3 · Make the header sticky. position: relative → sticky. Right now the burger is 3105px off-screen at the bottom of Services. S Fix: src/styles.css:47.

4 · Use the --row-cols you're already computing. Delete the hardcoded repeat(3, …) at editorial.css:771 and let wallCols() drive it. Goes to 2 columns at 393px (and 87px tiles → readable faces). S Fix: src/editorial.css:765-786.

Then

5 · LCP. Convert the 286 KB JPEG LCP image to WebP, and defer the 227.7 KB Supabase chunk off the critical path. 6.9 s → target under 2.5 s. M

6 · Lightbox. Serve a 1200w derivative, not the 406 KB full-res, and add swipe. M Fix: src/lightbox.ts.

7 · Menu scrim + outside-tap close + nav-open. Style the dead class and stop the background scrolling behind the open drawer. S Fix: src/main.ts:207.

8 · Contrast over photos. Hero eyebrow 1.78:1, burger 2.92:1, lede 3.35:1. Deepen the scrim behind them — WCAG AA needs 4.5:1. S Fix: src/styles.css hero rule.

9 · Type floor. Nothing below 15px; body to 16px. S Fix: src/styles.css scale block.

10 · Safe-area insets + viewport-fit=cover. The lightbox close button sits inside the Dynamic Island band on iPhone 16. S Fix: index.html + src/styles.css.

11 · prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme. M Fix: src/styles.css.

Housekeeping, no design involved

12 Add a www DNS record pointing at the apex. S 13 Turn on https_enforced for the Pages site. S 14 Make the old public Shutterhaus-Visuals repo private (masked phone + the booking-data leak from the September audit). S

Deliberately not recommending

No per-image focal points. The crop audit came back clean: 0/30 images lose any area, 0/30 deviate from their natural ratio, 0/30 have a subject outside the retained region. The full frame is always shown — you chose that over object-fit: cover from day one and it should stay. The face-size problem is a cell size problem, fixed by #4, not a crop problem.

No accent colour, no dark mode. Zero saturated colour site-wide is the right call for a monochrome photography brand, and the heading scale is disciplined (48→44→36→34→30→24→19, every step ≥1.14×). Leave both alone.
