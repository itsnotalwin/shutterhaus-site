# Shutterhaus public presentation

The 25 November 2026 desktop, mobile and brand boards guide the five public pages. Playfair Display headings and Montserrat text sit on a white page with fine rules, square buttons and a single tapered ink underline beneath “keeping.” The desktop Home pairs “Portraits worth keeping.” with a wider portrait and six manually controlled frames. The frame counter, progress line and arrows sit under the picture. On phones the headline precedes the photograph, arrows, introduction and booking button.

Every photograph comes from the Shutterhaus collection. The Home cover is Alwin’s windblown outdoor portrait. Home and Work present their four selections in 4:5 portrait frames, two across on phones and four across on desktop. Three portraits fill the frames. The landscape family photograph sits on a light grey mount so all six people are visible. Home’s captions and Work’s covers link to Portraits, On location, Families and Social content. Alwin shoots on location. Visitors can still enquire about couples photography.

The Home screenshots in this directory and in `src/` are full-page captures from 27 November 2026. [Desktop Home](SCREENSHOT.png) uses a 1440 × 900 viewport and [mobile Home](SCREENSHOT-MOBILE.png) uses 390 × 844. The 26 November Sessions captures show the package comparison and phone picker.
